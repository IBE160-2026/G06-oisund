# Human and AI process reflection

Jonas supplied the practical use case, source interpretation, scope decisions and acceptance decisions. AI assisted with requirements/specification, architecture, implementation, synthetic test tooling, documentation and review. The [development log](../development-log.md), per-user [session records](../../.logs/jonas-oisund/) and story evidence preserve the sequence and authority. This reflection reports that record; it does not estimate prompt counts, hours saved or percentages of authorship that were not measured.

The useful contribution was turning a large idea into checkable, explicit state transitions: import versus save, saved revision versus confirmation, planned versus actual time, source checking versus validity, and a missing reply versus an acknowledged write. Synthetic fixtures made real API/database testing possible without requiring the owner's private runtime. Independent expected facts reduce the risk of merely testing a parser against its own output.

AI output still required correction. The earlier scope included GPS/live feeds and broader capability than could be delivered responsibly within the course; Jonas approved the smaller SCOPE-02 V1. In 12.4, passing technical/device checks did not establish a satisfactory workflow: Jonas withheld acceptance because of repeated review, forced editing, bus-save friction and scrolling. The [correction report](../evidence/12-4-import-experience.md) records concrete changes, failed assertions, reruns and eventual [owner acceptance](../evidence/12-4-owner-acceptance-2026-10-28.md). This is evidence that human acceptance and automated green results answer different questions.

The accumulated README also became difficult to interpret: successive checkpoint statements contradicted later publication/acceptance status. Story 12.5 replaces that accumulation with one clean recipe while preserving original reports and Git history. A fresh dependency build, actual synthetic database, restart comparisons and wrong-target refusal tests check reproducibility instead of relying on an agent's claim that it should work.

The limits remain substantial. An independent test oracle can still omit requirements; browser automation does not prove comfortable long-term use; a small fixture set does not validate every PDF; several review agents can share assumptions. Tooling and AI review help expose concrete defects but cannot grant owner acceptance, establish field benefit, or remove the need to inspect source, environment and actual failures. No sustained real-driving study, measured time-saving result, full accessibility audit or new 12.5 hardware qualification is claimed.

The ethical and technical consequences meet in the data boundaries. A plausible AI interpretation of a timetable is still a proposal: the driver must inspect missing or uncertain facts before explicit confirmation. Planned clocks cannot establish arrival, manual completion cannot establish physical work, and an expired notice cannot establish road clearance. Synthetic demonstrations protect private accounts and documents while permitting repeatable checks; bounded extraction, fixed server retention, no private browser cache and reserved-account test guards make those choices enforceable. These controls reduce specific risks and do not certify use while driving.

During the12.5 manual walkthrough, Jonas also corrected the guidance to the visible **Fortsett til godkjenning** action and the observed **Lagret: Fullført manuelt** acknowledgement. These were corrections to the AI's instructions, not demonstrated product defects. Matching guidance to the actual interface mattered even after automated tests passed.

For this task, the owner authorized isolated implementation and evidence plus branch publication, while protecting existing services/data and withholding any implied merge/deploy/course submission. The [12.5 evidence](../evidence/12-5-reproduction.md) records the actual outcome and remaining uncertainties. After confirming all ten manual assessment parts, Jonas [explicitly accepted Story12.5 in full on28October2026](../evidence/12-5-owner-acceptance-2026-10-28.md). The decision does not qualify unperformed tests or close Epic12.
