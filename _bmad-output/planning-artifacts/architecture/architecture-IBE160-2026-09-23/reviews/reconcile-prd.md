# PRD reconciliation

Reviewed `prd.md`, its addendum and the current Architecture Spine against later user-approved decisions and relevant current UX overrides. Verdict: **substantially aligned; three small clarifications before finalization**. None requires reopening an adopted decision.

1. **Missing-stop passenger trips need an explicit completion exception in the contract/state-machine boundary.** Spine AD-7 retains unmatched items, but AD-9 says passenger trips finish at a registered final stop without stating the approved fallback. PRD FR-3, A-2 and EXPERIENCE failure paths explicitly permit manual completion/abortion and next-activity selection when a supported stop list cannot be recovered. This matters to event validation and summary provenance: such a trip has no final-stop ID to register. Add that unavailable stop lists disable automatic stop progression, retain known trip details, and permit the agreed manual completion/abort/next-activity flow without fabricating GPS/final-stop evidence. Locations: Spine AD-7/AD-9, approximately lines 68 and 90; PRD FR-3 and section 10 A-2.

2. **State the automatic-notice acceptance requirement unambiguously.** The Spine's source gate says missing coverage “may block” the capability, while PRD sections 5/9/10 and current EXPERIENCE require actual automatic Svipper-origin planned-notice retrieval and expressly reject manual/demo notices as a substitute. Candidate status and incomplete coverage are correctly qualified. Add one sentence distinguishing honest coverage limits from a non-working required capability: the actual automatic retrieval gate cannot be passed with manual/demo data, and failure returns to the owner for a scope/source decision. Locations: Spine AD-7 and Import and sources gate, approximately lines 70 and 253.

3. **Make the static demo's necessary simulation input boundary explicit.** The Spine correctly isolates the demo and mentions fictional fixtures/shared operational code, but does not explicitly bind its inputs to simulated position/speed and repeatable new-notice/network-loss/GPS-loss scenarios without physical GPS. FR-25 and current EXPERIENCE require those capabilities, including restart and visibly labelled exports. One compact sentence in Structural Seed plus a demo qualification item would prevent “fictional static display” from satisfying the described demo architecture. Reuse the operational engine with demo-only inputs; no general scenario platform is required. Locations: Spine Structural Seed near line 247 and release gates; PRD FR-25, EXPERIENCE Instructor simulation/UJ-2.

Correctly reconciled later overrides include: no-login fictional demo instead of the PRD's old test-login requirement; reliable movement above zero/immediate detail collapse instead of the old 6 km/h and 30-second policy; later five-minute loss behavior; immediate deletion of raw originals instead of seven-day source-document retention; own/linked plans and combined-day retention; PostgreSQL and the client-authoritative offline design. The addendum is historical support, not a competing unresolved-question register. Remaining UI detail and pilot observation methodology can stay in authoritative UX/PRD and downstream stories.
